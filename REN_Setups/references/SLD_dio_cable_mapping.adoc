
## Cable Mapping for the Soring Line with Detection (SLD)


### DIO_C
 
DIO side:

image::./img/dio-connectors.png[role="related thumb right", 240] 

[options="header",cols="1,1,1,1,3"]
|===
| DIO pin number |DIO var name | FT pin number   |FT Name   | FT Description    
//----------------------
|Output 1  |O_3   | 17 | Q1 |  motor converyor belt
|Output 2  |O_4   | 18 | Q2 |  compressor
|Output 3  |O_5   | 20 | Q3 |  valve first ejector (white)
|Output 4  |O_6   | 21 | Q4 |  valve second ejector (red)
|Output 5  |O_7   | 22 | Q5 |  valve third ejector (blue)
|Input 1   |I_1   | 5  | I1 | pulse counter
|Input 2   |I_2   | 6  | I2  | light-barrier inlet
|Input 3   |I_3   | 7  | I3 | light-barrier behind color sensor
|Input 5   |I_5   | 10 | I5 | light-barrier white
|Input 6   |I_6   | 11 | I6 | light-barrier red 
|Input 7   |I_7   | 12 | I7 | light-barrier blue
|===

Arduino connections on the DIO side:

[options="header",cols="1,1,1,1,4"]
|===
| DIO pin number | DIO var name | arduino pin | arduino Name | arduino Description
//----------------------
| Input 10 | I_10 | D3 | Green indicator | Send a logical 1 when an object/color is detected
| Input 11 | I_11 | D2 | Blue indicator | Send a logical 1 when the color blue is detected
| Input 12 | I_12 | D1 | Red indicator | Send a logical 1 when the color red is detected
| Input 13 | I_13 | D0 | White indicator | Send a logical 1 when the color white is detected
|===
