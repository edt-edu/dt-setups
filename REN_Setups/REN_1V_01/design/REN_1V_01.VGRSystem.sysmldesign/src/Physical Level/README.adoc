== Purpose: 

This SysML model of the vacuum gripper provides detailed physical-level descriptions, 
offering low-level details across all domains: electrical, hardware, mechanical, and pneumatic. 
The model aims to simulate commands and observe the precise effects on the token moved by the gripper, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the system's physical behavior.

== Scope: 

The model operates at the physical level, detailing the intricate interactions and components of the vacuum gripper. 
It includes exhaustive descriptions of electrical circuits, hardware components, mechanical movements, and pneumatic systems, providing an in-depth view of the system's functionality.

== Objectives:

- To simulate commands issued to the vacuum gripper with precise physical detail.

- To observe and analyze the interactions and positions of the token manipulated by the gripper with high fidelity.

- To provide a thorough understanding of the operational behavior of the vacuum gripper through detailed simulation.

- To specify the comprehensive physical architecture of the vacuum gripper, including the detailed separation of the machine and its controller.

- To present an in-depth view of the system's domains: electrical, hardware, mechanical, and pneumatic.

- To ensure that the model serves both prescriptive purposes (building the system and simulating expected behavior) and descriptive purposes (verifying that the built system behaves as expected).

== System Boundaries: 

The model encompasses the vacuum gripper, its controller, and the intricate interactions between them. 
It provides detailed views of the electrical circuits, hardware configurations, mechanical linkages, 
and pneumatic pathways, aiming to offer a complete understanding of the system's physical architecture and behavior.

== Context: 

This detailed model serves as a critical tool for physical-level analysis and validation of the vacuum gripper's functionality. 
It supports simulations to aid in design decisions, ensure accuracy in the construction of the system, and validate the precise 
behavior of the built system.
